**Analogizer support: the fight screen on a CRT, and the Pocket as the top monitor.**

Punch-Out!!, Super Punch-Out!! and Arm Wrestling now work with RndMnkIII's
[Analogizer](https://github.com/RndMnkIII/Analogizer) adapter. Confirmed on
a Pocket with an Analogizer and a CRT.

### Two monitors again

The cabinet had two stacked monitors. With the Analogizer:

- **The CRT gets the bottom monitor**: the fight screen alone, 256×224 at a
  15 kHz arcade monitor's own timing (15.31 kHz, 60.02 Hz), retimed from the
  core's picture, so it looks like the cabinet's lower screen rather than a
  slice of the Pocket's.
- **"Analogizer: On, Pocket: top screen"** turns the Pocket into the top
  monitor: the info screen fills its display. "On" keeps the Pocket's usual
  two-screen picture, and "On, Pocket off" blanks it.

### Set up from the core's menu

No `analogizer.bin` file needed. **Analogizer** (Off, On, On / Pocket: top
screen, On / Pocket off), **Analogizer Video** (RGBS, RGsB, YPbPr, Y/C NTSC
or PAL, scandoubled for a VGA monitor), **SNAC Adapter** and **SNAC
Assignment**, and **H / V Position** to centre the picture on the CRT (40
pixels left to 44 right, 17 lines up to 11 down). Off by default; with it off
the core plays exactly as before.

SNAC controllers (DB15, NES, SNES, PC Engine, PlayStation) play as the
Pocket's own controls.

### Menu changes

To make room, four entries left the menu and stay at their factory
settings: **Scanlines** and **Shadow Mask** (off), **Copyright Notice**
(Nintendo) and **Demo Sounds** (on).

### Before you install

**With this core the Pocket powers the cartridge slot.** Take any game
cartridge out first. Set the adapter up as its
[How to use it](https://github.com/RndMnkIII/Analogizer/wiki/How-to-use-it%3F)
page says (SNAC switch on A, 5 V into its USB-C port, the audio cable into
the Pocket's headphone socket).

### Installing

Unzip onto the SD card root, overwriting previous files. ROMs are not
included: build them with the included `mra_build.py` from your own MAME
romsets (`punchout.mra`, `spnchout.mra`, `armwrest.mra`) and put them in
`Assets/punchout/common/`.
